---
title: "Grant Access"
description: "Let a member or access group whose access scope is restricted reach a resource"
---

```
PUT /openapi/memorylake/admin/v1/access/{resource_type}/{resource_id}/scope-grants/{subject_type}/{subject_id}
```

Adds the resource to the access scope of a member or access group whose scope is restricted for this resource type, so they can reach it with the permissions they already have.

| `resource_type` | `resource_id` is | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| `workspaces` | `workspace_id` | `workspace:assign` on `workspace_id` or `member:set_scope` (when `subject_type` is `members`) or `access_group:manage` (when `subject_type` is `access-groups`)<br />Also required: `workspace:assign` on `workspace_id` or `access_group:view`<br />Also required: `workspace:read` on `workspace_id` |
| `projects` | `project_id` | `project:assign` on `project_id` or `member:set_scope` (when `subject_type` is `members`) or `access_group:manage` (when `subject_type` is `access-groups`)<br />Also required: `project:assign` on `project_id` or `access_group:view`<br />Also required: `project:read` on `project_id` |
| `actors` | `actor_id` | `actor:assign` on `actor_id` or `member:set_scope` (when `subject_type` is `members`) or `access_group:manage` (when `subject_type` is `access-groups`)<br />Also required: `actor:assign` on `actor_id` or `access_group:view`<br />Also required: `actor:read` on `actor_id` |
| `agents` | `agent_id` | `agent:assign` on `agent_id` or `member:set_scope` (when `subject_type` is `members`) or `access_group:manage` (when `subject_type` is `access-groups`)<br />Also required: `agent:assign` on `agent_id` or `access_group:view`<br />Also required: `agent:read` on `agent_id` |
| `skills` | `skill_id` | `skill:assign` on `skill_id` or `member:set_scope` (when `subject_type` is `members`) or `access_group:manage` (when `subject_type` is `access-groups`)<br />Also required: `skill:assign` on `skill_id` or `access_group:view`<br />Also required: `skill:read` on `skill_id` |
| `boundaries` | `boundary_id` | `boundary:assign` on `boundary_id` or `member:set_scope` (when `subject_type` is `members`) or `access_group:manage` (when `subject_type` is `access-groups`)<br />Also required: `boundary:assign` on `boundary_id` or `access_group:view`<br />Also required: `boundary:read` on `boundary_id` |
| `workspaces/{workspace_id}/actors` | `actor_id` | `workspace_actor:assign` on `workspace_id}/{actor_id` or `member:set_scope` (when `subject_type` is `members`) or `access_group:manage` (when `subject_type` is `access-groups`)<br />Also required: `workspace_actor:assign` on `workspace_id}/{actor_id` or `access_group:view`<br />Also required: `workspace_actor:read` on `workspace_id}/{actor_id` |
| `workspaces/{workspace_id}/agents` | `agent_id` | `workspace_agent:assign` on `workspace_id}/{agent_id` or `member:set_scope` (when `subject_type` is `members`) or `access_group:manage` (when `subject_type` is `access-groups`)<br />Also required: `workspace_agent:assign` on `workspace_id}/{agent_id` or `access_group:view`<br />Also required: `workspace_agent:read` on `workspace_id}/{agent_id` |

### Path Parameters

<ParamField path="resource_type" type="string" required>
Resource type — one of the values in the table above
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="resource_id" type="string" required>
Identifier of the resource, as listed in the table above
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="subject_type" type="string" required>
members or access-groups
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="subject_id" type="string" required>
principal_id of a member, or an access group id
</ParamField>

### Headers

<ParamField header="Idempotency-Key" type="string">
Optional. Retrying with the same value replays the first result.
</ParamField>

### Request Body

<ParamField body="reason" type="string" required>
Why the change is made; recorded in the audit log
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="workspace_id" type="string">
Projects only: the project's workspace, so the project can be shown by name in access scopes
</ParamField>
