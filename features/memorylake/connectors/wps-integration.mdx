## Overview

WPS (Kingsoft Office) integration lets you browse your WPS documents directly within MemoryLake. This guide walks through the setup — Lark (Feishu) works [the same way](#lark-feishu-works-the-same-way).

## Prerequisites

- An active WPS account
- You are working in your **personal space** — connectors are disabled in team and organization workspaces

## Step-by-Step Setup

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the WPS Connector">
Navigate to the Connectors section in MemoryLake and click the WPS connector card.

{/* SCREENSHOT: WPS connector card in connectors section */}
</Step>

<Step title="Authorize in the Popup">
A popup window opens WPS's authorization page. Sign in with your WPS credentials and approve the access request.

<Tip>
Ensure pop-ups are enabled for MemoryLake. The OAuth flow uses a popup window; the result is passed back to the console automatically when you finish.
</Tip>

<Note>
MemoryLake requests read-only access. We cannot modify or delete your WPS documents, and your WPS password never leaves WPS.
</Note>
</Step>

<Step title="Browse WPS Space">
Once connected, a **WPS Space** node appears in your library tree. Expand it to browse your WPS documents — folder contents are fetched from WPS on demand as you expand them.

{/* SCREENSHOT: WPS Space folder in the library tree */}

<Check>
The "WPS Space" folder appears in your library and expands to show your WPS content.
</Check>
</Step>

</Steps>

## Working with WPS Documents

- **Browse**: navigate WPS folders like regular MemoryLake folders.
- **Download**: download copies of WPS files for local use (changes to local copies don't sync back).
- **Associate with projects**: open a project → Files → add documents → navigate into WPS Space and select files or folders. AI assistants connected to the project can then read that content.

WPS-mounted content is **read-only** — you can browse, download, and associate it with projects, but not upload, edit, rename, move, or delete through MemoryLake.

<Tip>
To modify WPS documents, use the WPS application directly. Changes appear in MemoryLake when you re-expand or refresh the folder.
</Tip>

## Reconnecting

If your WPS authorization expires, expanding WPS Space prompts you to re-authorize — one OAuth round trip and you're back. To disconnect entirely, use the connector card's disconnect action; this unmounts WPS Space and revokes MemoryLake's access.

<Warning>
Disconnecting removes the WPS mount. WPS files associated with projects will become inaccessible to those projects until you reconnect.
</Warning>

## Lark (Feishu) Works the Same Way

The Lark connector follows exactly the same flow: click the **Lark** connector card, sign in through the popup, and a **Lark Space** node appears in your library tree with the same read-only browsing, download, and project-association capabilities.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="OAuth window doesn't open">
**Solutions**:
- Enable pop-ups for the MemoryLake domain
- Check browser popup blocker settings
- Try a different browser, or temporarily disable extensions
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Connector card is grayed out">
Connectors are only available in your personal space. If you are in a team or organization workspace, switch to your personal space. If it is still unavailable, the connector may not be enabled for your deployment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Space shows stale content">
Contents are fetched when folders expand. Collapse and re-expand the folder, or refresh the page, to pick up changes made in the external service.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Security & Privacy

- **OAuth 2.0**: industry-standard secure authorization
- **Read-Only**: MemoryLake cannot modify your WPS content
- **Revocable**: disconnect anytime from the connector card
- **No Password Storage**: your WPS password never leaves WPS
