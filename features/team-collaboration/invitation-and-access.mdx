## Use Cases
- Bring colleagues into a shared workspace so team memory and quotas are collective.
- Resend or revoke invitations in a controlled way to avoid mistakes or stale links.
- Assign the right role at invitation time rather than fixing permissions afterwards.

<Note>
All new members join through **invitation and acceptance** — there is no direct add-member action. Both sides need a **verified email address**: if yours isn't verified yet, the console prompts you to add and verify it before sending invitations, and invitees must have a verified address to accept.
</Note>

## How to Invite

- **Email invitation**: enter one or more email addresses on the team's **Invites** tab. You can paste a batch — one address per line — and specify a role per line. Invitations can be resent or revoked while pending.
- **Invitation link**: generate a shareable link. Anyone opening it can accept after signing in (or after completing registration). Revoke the link to invalidate it.
- **Invitee experience**: accepting after sign-in adds them to the team with the role you assigned. If they open an invite while logged out, they're returned to it automatically after signing in or registering.

## Joining and Access Control

- **Before acceptance**: pending invitations can be revoked at any time. Wrong address? Revoke and resend — email matching is case- and whitespace-insensitive, so duplicates are caught.
- **After acceptance**: adjust roles and quotas from the **Members** tab, grant per-resource scopes, or remove members. Members can also leave voluntarily.
- **Workspace access**: new members are added to the team's workspaces automatically, so shared memory is immediately available (deployment-configurable).
- **Context and permissions**: once in the team, calls made in the team context draw on team quota and follow the member's role permissions.

## Quick Start

1. Open team details → **Invites**, and send an email invitation (or generate a link).
2. Assign each invitee's role as you invite — Owner, Admin, Member, or a custom role.
3. Revoke or resend anything still pending as needed.
4. After they join, fine-tune quotas and per-resource scopes on the **Members** tab.

## Tips

- Revoked invitations and links are permanently invalid — generate a new one to continue.
- For sensitive teams, prefer email invitations (identity is verified) and revoke links after short-term use.
- Confirm your current space before making calls, so you don't consume personal quota in a team context or vice versa.
